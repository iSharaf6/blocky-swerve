Blocky Swerve Support

Questions, bugs or a purchase that did not arrive? Email islamsharaf2005@gmail.com and say which phone you play on.

I bought something on another phone

Open the STORE and tap RESTORE PURCHASES (bottom left). Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I steer by tilting the phone?

Settings, then TOUCH, then TILT. If the phone asks to use motion, allow it. You can also pick arrow buttons, a thumb stick, or drag to steer.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Deleting the app deletes your progress, but anything you bought comes back with RESTORE PURCHASES.

The game feels slow

Settings, then SCREEN, then QUALITY: try LOW. AUTO lowers it by itself when the phone struggles, for example in Low Power Mode.

Which languages can I play in?

26: English, Español, Português, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Türkçe, Bahasa Indonesia, Polski, Nederlands, Svenska, Dansk, Bahasa Melayu, Русский, Українська, Ελληνικά, العربية, हिन्दी, বাংলা, தமிழ், ไทย, Tiếng Việt, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文 and 繁體中文. The game follows your phone's language; to pick another, go to Settings, then SCREEN, then LANGUAGE.
