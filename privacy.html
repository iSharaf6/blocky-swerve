Blocky Swerve: Privacy Policy

Who we are

Blocky Swerve is made by Calynx. Contact: islamsharaf2005@gmail.com

What the game stores

Your progress (cars, coins, settings and so on) is saved on your own device. In the iPhone app it never leaves the phone. We have no accounts, no chat, and no way to contact other players.

What we collect

We (Calynx) receive nothing about you: the game never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or microphone, and sends us no information about how you play. In the iPhone app, two features use other companies, described below: the ads (Google) and INVITE FRIENDS (Apple's iCloud).

Ads (iPhone app)

The iPhone app shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad between drives, and an ad you choose to watch for a bonus. To show them, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the app asks), an approximate location worked out from your internet address, which ads you saw and tapped, and crash and performance data. In the EU and the UK the app asks for your consent first; you can change it later in Settings, HELP, AD PRIVACY, and tracking can be turned off at any time in your iPhone's Settings, Privacy, Tracking. Ads are capped at Google's PG (parental guidance) content rating: nothing rated for teens or adults. Google's use of this data is covered by its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Buying NO ADS stops the ads between drives.

Saw an ad that should not be there? In the app go to Settings, HELP, REPORT AN AD (it opens an email to us), or email islamsharaf2005@gmail.com with what the ad showed and roughly when. We look into every report and can block an advertiser from the game through Google AdMob.

Invite friends (iPhone app)

If you use INVITE FRIENDS, the game keeps your six character invite code, and a note when someone uses a code, in the game's own iCloud (CloudKit) database run by Apple. These records carry only the code and an anonymous identifier that Apple gives the app for your iCloud account: no name, email or contact details. They are used only to give out the invite gifts.

Motion sensors

If you choose TILT steering, the game reads the phone's tilt to steer the car. That reading is used at once and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases (iPhone app)

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. Apple tells the app which items you own so it can unlock them and restore them on a new phone. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles your account.

Playing on a web portal

When you play on a site such as CrazyGames, that site shows the advertising and, if you sign in there, keeps your progress in your account with them. The site's own privacy policy covers what it collects. The game itself gives the site only what it needs to work: when a drive starts and stops, when an ad may be shown, and your saved progress.

Other companies

The only other companies that receive any data through the game are Google (the ads) and Apple (purchases and iCloud). Each provides the same or equal protection of that data as this policy, under its own privacy policy and its terms with us, and uses it only for the purposes described here.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

We (Calynx) keep no data about you. Your progress stays on your phone until you delete the app or use Settings, HELP, RESET SAVE. INVITE FRIENDS records stay in the game's iCloud database while the invite gifts can still be claimed; to have yours deleted, email islamsharaf2005@gmail.com with your invite code and they are deleted within 30 days. Google keeps ad data for the time set out in its own policy; turning off tracking in your iPhone's Settings stops the advertising identifier being shared from then on.

Age rating

Blocky Swerve is rated 9+ and made for a general audience; it is not directed at children under 13. We collect no personal information from anyone. Ads are personalised only with permission and capped at a PG rating, and every purchase has a short check (a times table question) before Apple's purchase sheet, so nothing is bought by accident.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date.
